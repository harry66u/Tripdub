# Tripdub: Design Spec

Date: 2026-11-03. Status: draft for review.

Tripdub is a web-first daily game site with three games: **Starting Five** (featured), **Daily Trivia**, and **Shot Chart**. It is built for desktop browsers first and must also play well on phones. All input is tap/click or drag; there is no typing during gameplay.

This spec is the product brief from the request plus the decisions made since. Anything marked **Assumption** is my call and can be changed.

---

## 1. Decisions so far

| Topic | Decision |
|---|---|
| Starting Five agency | **Matchups + 1 re-spin.** The slot you choose decides who that player guards and attacks in the sim. Each mystery-team slot shows a clue. One re-spin per game lets you pass on a player. Spin order is the same for everyone. |
| Stack | Next.js (App Router, TypeScript) on **Vercel**; **Supabase** for Postgres, Google auth, and Realtime. |
| Daily reset | **Assumption:** one global puzzle day, resetting at midnight America/New_York. Everyone plays the same puzzle on the same leaderboard. |
| Streak | **Assumption:** the overall streak continues on any day you finish at least one daily game. A day with all three finished is a **Tripdub**; Tripdub days get a special marker, a celebration, and a separate Tripdub count. |
| Anonymous play | Results are kept in localStorage. On Google sign-in they are uploaded, validated by the server, and attached to the account. |

## 2. Architecture

```
Browser (Next.js client components)
  ├─ game UIs (wheel, trivia, shot chart), motion animations
  ├─ localStorage for anonymous results
  └─ Supabase JS (auth session, Realtime channels for 1v1)
        │
Next.js route handlers on Vercel (server, authoritative)
  ├─ /api/daily/*   serve today's puzzle progressively; answers withheld
  ├─ /api/submit    recompute and validate the score, write the result
  ├─ /api/claim     attach anonymous results after sign-in
  ├─ /api/match/*   1v1 create / join / progress / finish
  └─ reads content/ (schedule) + src/data/ (generated datasets) server-side only
        │
Supabase Postgres (RLS on every table)
  profiles, daily_results, daily_runs, question_stats, matches, match_players, match_queue
```

Rules:
- **Game engines are pure TypeScript** (`src/games/<game>/engine/`), shared by client and server and unit-tested with Vitest. The server always recomputes scores; the client's number is only for display.
- **Answers never ship to the client before they are earned.** Future spins, the mystery team, trivia answers, and shot chart identities are served one step at a time.
- **Content and datasets live in the repo** as JSON, generated by scripts under `scripts/`. No paid APIs at runtime.

There are no logos or team marks anywhere. **Player headshots:** official NBA.com headshots, loaded directly from cdn.nba.com by NBA.com person id (your decision, 2026-11-03; the NBA owns these images, so a takedown request is possible). If a headshot is missing, the player's varsity initials show instead. Photos appear only after a player is revealed (a card after the spin, results, Shot Chart reveals), never before a guess.

The pipeline is `scripts/data/*.py` (download, clean, aggregate) writing compact JSON to `src/data/`. Raw CSVs are not committed. `npm run data` re-runs everything.

## 4. Game 1: Starting Five

### 4.1 Player pool
- About 500–700 notable NBA players from every era. Eligibility: real NBA careers with meaningful minutes, and at least one of All-Star, All-NBA, All-Defense, a major award, or a high career Win Shares total. The pool also includes some strong role players so spins vary.
- Each player is represented by their **prime**: their best seasons by Win Shares (up to 5, each with at least 1,000 minutes), averaged with minutes weighting. The card shows the real prime per-game line (PTS/REB/AST/STL/BLK, 3P%, FT%, TS%), the prime span (for example "Prime 1987–1993"), natural position, height, and accolades.
- **Tiers** (Legend / All-Star / Starter / Role) come from an overall rating and set wheel segment colors and the daily spin mix.

### 4.2 Ratings (0–99)
Each stat is converted to an **era-relative z-score** against qualified players in the same season, then averaged across the prime. This handles pace, rule, and era differences (a 1960s rebound total is compared to 1960s peers). Ratings are Tripdub's model; every stat shown on a card is real.

| Rating | Inputs (era-relative) | Fallback when a stat wasn't tracked yet |
|---|---|---|
| Scoring | points per 100 possessions or per 36, TS% above league average | per 36 and TS% only |
| Playmaking | AST%, assists per 36, low TOV% | assists per 36 |
| Rebounding | TRB%, ORB% | rebounds per 36 relative to the season |
| Perimeter D | STL%, DBPM, All-Defense, DPOY shares | DWS/48 + All-Defense + height |
| Rim protection | BLK%, height, DBPM | height + DWS/48 + rebounding (blocks weren't tracked before 1973-74) |
| Shooting / spacing | 3PA rate × 3P% above league, FT% | FT% only (pre-1979-80 players have no 3PT; touch comes from FT%) |

Each player also has a **shot profile** (share of shots at the rim, mid-range, and three) from Basketball-Reference shooting splits (1996-97 on), estimated from position and 3PA rate for earlier players.

### 4.3 Lineup score: the five dimensions
These are lineup-level grades (0–100) shown as bars and letter grades. They reward composition, not names:
- **Scoring:** top-heavy weights (best scorer 40%, then 27/18/10/5) to model "one ball."
- **Playmaking:** driven by the best playmaker (60/25/15). No real playmaker means a low grade.
- **Rebounding:** weighted top three plus everyone's contribution.
- **Spacing:** counts capable shooters. Every non-shooter drags the grade down.
- **Defense:** half perimeter (the top three perimeter defenders) and half rim protection (the best rim protector). No rim protector caps the grade.

### 4.4 Daily flow
1. The page shows the **Mystery Five**: five slots (PG–C), each with a clue such as "2010s · Sharpshooter" or "1980s · Rim Protector". The team is revealed at the end.
2. Spin. The wheel shows ~16 name segments (the predetermined player plus decoys from the pool), spins smoothly, eases out with ticks, pauses as it nears its stop, then lands and reveals the player card.
3. Put the player in any open slot (tap the slot, or drag the card onto it). A filled slot is locked. Alternatively, use the **one re-spin** to pass; the next player in today's sequence appears. A daily sequence has 6 players.
4. After 5 placements, **Simulate**.

### 4.5 Simulation and score
- A possession-level sim with a seeded RNG, so the same lineup on the same day always gets the same result. Each slot is matched against the same slot on the other team, both ways. Shot selection follows the shooter's profile. Make probability depends on the shooter's scoring and shooting, the matched defender (perimeter D for jumpers, rim protection plus team help for rim shots), and team spacing and playmaking. Rebounding drives second chances, and playmaking lowers turnovers.
- Matchups are a mismatch in the stats, not an out-of-position penalty. A center placed at PG has no label penalty; he guards their PG, and if that player is a sharpshooter, his perimeter-D numbers decide how it goes.
- The engine runs 400 seeded sims:
  - **Win Probability** = P(win) from the sims' margin mean and standard deviation. It is the **leaderboard score** (one decimal), with expected margin as the tiebreaker.
  - The game shown is the sim **closest to the median margin**, so the final score always agrees with the win probability.
- Results screen: final score, quarter-by-quarter line, box score (PTS/REB/AST/STL/BLK per player), the five dimension grades, the Mystery Five reveal, and a short generated **game summary** (3–4 sentences built from the box score, for example the top scorer, the key matchup, and the stat that decided it).
- Global daily leaderboard (signed-in players) and a percentile for everyone ("Top 12% today"). Anonymous players see the percentile and a sign-in prompt to get on the board.

### 4.6 Anti-cheat (proportionate to a casual game)
Daily spins are served one at a time by `/api/daily/starting-five/step` using HMAC-signed run tokens, so future spins aren't in the page payload. The server re-validates the full action history and recomputes the score on submit. For signed-in players the run state is stored in `daily_runs`, so they can't fork. Accepted risk: an anonymous player could fork tokens with crafted requests and then claim the result after sign-in.

## 5. Home screen
- Brand header, a countdown to the next reset, and the overall streak flame with today's Tripdub progress (three pips).
- **Starting Five** hero card first and largest; **Daily Trivia** and **Shot Chart** cards below. Each card shows today's status (Play / In progress / Done with result) and a **1v1** button.
- When all three are done: a **Tripdub celebration** (confetti burst, triple-stripe animation) and a combined share button.

## 6. Accounts and profile
- Supabase Auth with Google. The sign-in prompt appears at natural moments: after a result ("Save this and get on the leaderboard"), on 1v1, and in the header.
- On sign-in, local results for today and earlier are posted to `/api/claim`, validated, and inserted. Duplicates are ignored, and existing server rows win.
- Everything is stored per account: daily results (score plus a detail blob), streaks (derived), 1v1 record, match history, and stats.
- **Profile page:** current and best streak, Tripdub count, best scores per game, 1v1 W/L per game, and per-game history (calendar strip plus a list of past results).

## 7. Game 2: Daily Trivia
- 7 questions a day, 4 options each, in rising difficulty (2 easy, 3 medium, 2 hard). 15-second timer per question.
- Points: correct = 100 + up to 100 speed bonus (linear in time remaining) + **rarity bonus** up to 75, scaled by how many players missed it (from `question_stats` at answer time).
- After each answer: right/wrong and "**41% of players got this right**". Until a question has 20 answers it shows "Early bird: one of the first N to answer" to avoid a misleading percentage. Anonymous answers count toward the percentage, deduplicated per device.
- The server serves one question at a time and checks the answer, so correct answers aren't in the payload ahead of time.

## 8. Game 3: Shot Chart
- 5 rounds a day. Each shows a real player-season shot chart in 14 zones (restricted area, paint, 5 mid-range, 2 corner threes, 3 above-the-break) on an original court drawing. Zone **color** is FG% relative to that season's league average in that zone (cold to hot). Zone **intensity/size** shows volume share. Name hidden.
- Choose from 4 names. Difficulty rises: round 1 is a star season (MVP/All-NBA level), round 5 is a deep cut (meaningful minutes, never an All-Star). Distractors come from the same season with similar position and shot profile, so the right answer can't be guessed by elimination.
- After each round: reveal the player, season, team name as text, and the per-game line.
- Score: correct answers plus a speed bonus; the share line is an emoji row.

## 9. 1v1 (all three games)
- Separate from the dailies, with fresh content per match from a seeded generator that excludes the next 30 days of daily content.
- **Challenge a friend:** create a match and share the link. **Random:** join a queue and get paired with the next waiting player for that game.
- Both sides get identical content. **Live** when both are online: Supabase Realtime presence plus broadcast shows the opponent's progress (their current spin or question and their score so far) as it happens. **Async** otherwise: each side plays on their own time, and results appear when both finish (with a notification on the home screen).
- Starting Five 1v1: both lineups play each other in the sim (slot vs. slot) for a final score and summary. Trivia and Shot Chart 1v1: same rounds, scored on accuracy and speed.
- W/L record per game, match history, and a **Rematch** button (new content, same opponent).

## 10. Sharing
Every result has a **Share** button that copies spoiler-free text (and uses the Web Share API on phones):
```
TRIPDUB · Starting Five #12
W 118–109 vs the Mystery Five · Win prob 78%
SCO 🟧 DEF 🟦 REB 🟩 SPC 🟨 PLY 🟥
tripdub.app
```
Trivia shows an emoji row per question (⚡ fast correct, 🟩 correct, 🟥 miss). Shot Chart shows 🎯/❌ per round. When all three are done there is a combined **Tripdub** share, and 1v1 results have their own share line.

## 11. Content scheduling
- `content/schedule/<game>.json` maps each date to that day's puzzle (Starting Five: spin ids, mystery ids, theme; Trivia: question ids; Shot Chart: player-season ids and distractors).
- `npm run schedule` fills the next **730 days** without overwriting existing dates, so manual edits stay. If a date is ever missing, the server generates it deterministically from the date seed, so there is always a puzzle.
- Rules the generator enforces: no repeated Starting Five spin player within 45 days, a mystery team theme rotation, no trivia question reuse, and no shot chart player-season reuse.
- Editing: change the JSON directly, or use `npm run puzzle -- show|set|reroll <game> <date>`. `npm run schedule:check` validates ids and runs in CI. An admin-only `/admin` page (gated by your email) previews upcoming puzzles.

## 12. Design direction
- Built from the Tripdub icon set (coral ball with navy outline on sky blue; variants: retro drop shadow, three stars, varsity 3). Light theme, colors sampled from the icons: wash `#EFF5FA`, sky `#9BC7EE`, navy ink `#1B3963`, coral `#ED7868`, white. Small supporting tones: butter `#FFCF5C` (legend tier, stars) and mint `#5BC9A0` (correct answers).
- Illustrated, retro-athletic style: consistent navy outlines, **hard offset drop shadows** (no blur) on interactive surfaces, generous rounded corners like the app-icon squircle, and flat fills. Buttons press "into" their shadow.
- Type: **Graduate** (collegiate varsity) for the wordmark, slot letters, and scoreboard numerals. **Bricolage Grotesque** (variable) for headings and UI. Self-hosted via Fontsource.
- Brand marks: the three-stars ball is the header logo (three games), the drop-shadow ball is the favicon/app icon, and the varsity 3 appears in the Tripdub celebration and the combined share.
- Motion: the wheel uses physics-style easing with tick feedback and a slowdown near its stop. Card flip reveals, slot snap with a spring, and score count-ups. `prefers-reduced-motion` is respected.
- Performance: server components for shells, game code split per route, no large client bundles, and fonts subset.

## 13. Testing
- Vitest unit tests for the rating pipeline outputs (sanity checks on known players), lineup grading, sim determinism, the median-game selection, schedule generation and validation, scoring for every game, and share text.
- Playwright smoke tests for each game flow on desktop and phone viewports, plus screenshots for visual checks.
